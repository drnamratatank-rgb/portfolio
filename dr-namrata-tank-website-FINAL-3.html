My Journey

From caring for people to thinking about the care around them.

My professional journey began with clinical practice. It taught me something I continue to carry into everything I do: listen before you decide. A person is never just a diagnosis — there is a family, a lifestyle, a workplace, a mind, a story, a fear, a hope, and often a need that has not yet been expressed. These questions gradually took my work beyond the consultation room, towards healthcare entrepreneurship, management, service development and collaboration.

  • Who supports a person after surgery?
  • What happens when someone returns home after a hospital stay?
  • How can healthcare professionals work better together?

The Way I Work

I care. I understand. I connect.

I Care

I start with the person, not the diagnosis.

My clinical work is built around listening, understanding and personal involvement. Sometimes treatment is the answer. Sometimes it's education, family guidance, or simply time and reassurance. The same condition affects two people very differently — so that's where I start.

I Understand

I design for people and systems, together.

Good healthcare cannot depend only on individual effort — it needs the right people, the right process, clear communication and coordination. Technology has a role here too, when it genuinely improves access and continuity of care. But it should support healthcare, never replace the human connection at its centre.

I Connect

I bring the right people together to build it.

A doctor brings clinical knowledge. A nurse brings care. A hospital brings infrastructure. A family brings understanding of the person. An organisation brings resources. A community brings trust. I connect them at the right time, to build something none of them could alone.

Health is not only about what happens inside a clinic or hospital.

It is also shaped by how we live, what we understand, what we prevent, the support around us and the choices we are able to make — which is why community health, awareness and honest conversations about wellbeing matter to me too.

Healthcare
"The right care should reach the person at the right time, in the right place — with the right human involvement." Restoring hope, at home.

Healthcare does not begin at the hospital, and it does not end when treatment is over. My experience with people and families led me to create Neev Healthcare around that simple belief.

Neev is growing as a person-centred healthcare platform supporting people across different stages of their journey — the need may change, but the person always remains at the centre.
